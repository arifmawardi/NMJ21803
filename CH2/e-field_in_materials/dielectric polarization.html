Polarization (Normal State)

  • Polarization: In an external electric field (→), a dielectric becomes polarized, meaning its atoms become streched.
  • Dielectric Constant (κ): Higher κ materials polarize more easily in an electric field., Therefore κ is a measure of how easily to polarize a material.
  • Induced Field (Eind): The polarization creates an internal electric field (←) that strictly opposes the applied field (→).
  • Net Field (Enet): The overall field (→) inside is weakened: Enet = E0 / κ.

Dielectric Breakdown

  • Dielectric Strength: The maximum electric field a material can withstand before ionizing.
  • Ionization: When E0 exceeds this limit, electrons are violently ripped from their nuclei.
  • Conduction: The insulator fails and becomes a conductor. A massive current (sparks/arcs) flows, destroying the polarization effect.
