Atomic Polarization in an Electric Field

Observe how an external electric field displaces the nucleus and the electron cloud of an atom, creating an induced electric dipole.

Mechanism

In the absence of an external electric field, the center of the negative electron cloud coincides with the positive nucleus. When an external field (E) is applied, the positive nucleus is pushed in the direction of the field, while the negative electron cloud is pulled in the opposite direction.
