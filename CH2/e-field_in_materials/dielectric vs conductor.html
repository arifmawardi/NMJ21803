Electric Fields in Materials

Observe the fundamental differences in electronic structure. In dielectrics, electrons are bound in localized clouds, while in conductors, they form a "sea" of free electrons.

Dielectric (Insulator)

Bound electron clouds

Behavior: Electrons form a localized cloud around each positive nucleus.

Conductor

Free electron gas

Behavior: Valence electrons detach from their atoms and roam freely.
