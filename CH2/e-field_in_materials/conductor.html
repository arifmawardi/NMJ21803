Conductors in Electric Fields

Observe how perfectly conducting shapes distort a uniform electric field. Drag the shape to interact!

Physics Highlights

  • • At the surface: The electric field is always normal (perpendicular) to the surface of a conductor.
  • • Inside the conductor: In electrostatic equilibrium, the electric field is zero everywhere inside.
  • • Internal charge: The net electric charge inside a solid conductor is zero.
  • • Charge distribution: Any excess charge resides entirely on the outer surface of the conductor.
